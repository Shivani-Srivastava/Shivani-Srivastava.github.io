SHIVANI SRIVASTAVA
Senior Data Scientist — New Delhi, India

I collect strange questions and try to answer them with data. 5+ years building applied machine learning, GenAI, and behavioral-analytics systems across financial services, executive research, and environmental policy — risk models influencing collections workflows worth roughly $950M, early-warning systems built from satellite imagery, causal-inference studies on public policy interventions.

At Genpact I lead a team of 5 data scientists building risk classification, propensity-to-pay, and contact-strategy models across 6 enterprise clients. Before that, at ISB, I ran behavioral research spanning eye-tracking studies, long-form interviews, and Bayesian text analysis, while mentoring 12 interns.

My roots are in economics — an MSc in Environmental and Natural Resource Economics, with field research spanning air-pollution early-warning systems to causal evaluation of policy interventions.
